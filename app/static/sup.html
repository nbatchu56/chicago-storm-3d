    Before you launch

    • Offshore wind is the main danger. A west wind makes the water look flat at the beach but pushes boards out into the lake. The forecast flags it.
    • Wear a PFD and a leash. The Coast Guard counts a SUP as a vessel outside swimming and surfing areas.
    • Seawalls and revetments at North Avenue, Oak Street and along the downtown shore bounce waves back. Expect confused chop there, more than the numbers suggest.
    • Check the official NWS nearshore forecast and beach status before you go. Conditions change fast on Lake Michigan.
