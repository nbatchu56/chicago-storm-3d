## Decisions

- Flatten buildings rather than hide them for the drone view: keeps the city as a footprint map; a fade would need
  transparent sorting. Why: cheapest honest read of the river's walls, bridges and water from above.
- Pitch nudge only for panned arrivals: presets and double-clicks are deliberate views.
- Compact strip is the default; "details" is per device. Why: the user asked for auto-minimize with only the paddling
  light and the compass kept.
- Kayak Chicago's container drawn too, in grey, so the two outfits read apart. The user only asked for Chicago SUP's.
- The reel films the page itself (canvas readback on a virtual clock) instead of screen capture: Playwright screenshots
  cost 16–35 s each under software GL; canvas toDataURL after a hand-driven frame costs ~1.5–3 s.
- Storm scene uses the Jul 27 2026 severe storms, not the Aug 11 derecho: at the derecho's peak the camera is inside
  the rain volume and the natural view is near-black (overcast 1.0); radar mode shows the cell from outside on Jul 27.
- Music synthesized (numpy) rather than sourced: no licensing; cannot be auditioned here.
